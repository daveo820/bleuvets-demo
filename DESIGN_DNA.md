# DESIGN_DNA: Bleuvets (Dunlap ranch, Austin) concept

**One line:** a private ranch with a movie room, sold like a night out: the ticket, the marquee, the curtain.

| Choice | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero archetype | Full bleed dusk photo (deck under string lights) with a notched ticket card holding the h1 and six facts, plus a rotated Airbnb score tag | The theater room is the hook, the yard at dusk is the mood. Not used in recent demos (Edgewood scorecard collage, Ed Briggs type led). |
| Display / body | Fraunces 500/700 + Figtree 400/600/700, self hosted | Warm hospitality serif with a crisp UI sans. |
| Palette | `--bleu` #14213d, `--curtain` #a3262a, `--brass` #c9a24a, `--lawn` #55703a, `--cream` #f6f1e7, `--linen` #ece4d3, `--muted` #5d584f | Bleu from the brand name, curtain red and brass from the theater photos. |
| Signature | The ticket stub: notched card edges, dashed perforation, a stub icon on every kicker, rule and bed card | Plays off the home theater. |
| Motion | Editorial stillness: 0.8s fade and rise, reduced motion honored | |
| Grid break | Home: the score tag hangs off the hero, the kitchen photo bleeds left in the mosaic. House: rotated dining photo. | |
| Footer | Closing spread: "Book the whole ranch." with the phone at display size | |
