Getting here

The ranch is in Del Valle on the east side of Austin. The host lists it about 10 minutes from Austin Bergstrom airport and Circuit of the Americas, close to Tesla Giga Texas, and roughly 15 to 20 minutes from downtown.

House rules, in short

  1. One group at a time. The whole property is yours.
  2. Up to 14 overnight guests, all disclosed in advance.
  3. Pets with approval, $150 each per stay.
  4. No unapproved parties or events.
  5. Park in the garage or driveway only.
  6. No smoking or vaping indoors.
  7. Quiet hours, kept with a noise monitor that records no audio.

Questions

How many people can stay?

Up to 14 overnight guests. The nightly rate covers 6, and each extra guest from number 7 is $55 a night. Every overnight guest and visitor has to be disclosed in advance.

Are pets allowed?

House trained pets are welcome with approval. The fee is $150 per pet, per stay. Pets stay off the beds and furniture, and waste gets picked up.

Can we host an event or a shoot?

Only with written approval first. Events, gatherings, commercial photography and filming may carry extra fees, parking rules and a separate agreement. Unapproved parties are not allowed.

Where do we park?

In the garage or the driveway. Street parking is not allowed. Tell Bleuvets before you book if you are bringing several cars.

What are check in and check out times?

Check in is 4 pm and check out is 10 am on the Bleuvets booking portal. Early check in and late check out depend on availability and need approval.

Are there cameras?

Seven exterior cameras record the deck, entrance walkway, rear fence, backyard, barn fence, driveway and front yard. There are none inside. A noise monitor measures decibel levels only and records no audio.

How is the internet?

Starlink satellite is the only service out here. It handles normal browsing and streaming, though weather can affect it.

Can we smoke?

Not indoors, including vaping. Outdoors is fine if butts are disposed of properly.
